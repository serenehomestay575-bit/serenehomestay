Our Story

A Working Farm With Rooms for Guests

Serene Homestay sits on farmland in Hirenasabi, a short drive from Badami's red sandstone hills. Whether you're here to explore the Chalukya-era temples nearby or simply do nothing at all, we keep the pace unhurried.

Every stay includes access to open fields and picnic seating. Bonfire evenings and meals are available as add-ons on request.
